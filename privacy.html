Privacy notice

Last updated: November 6, 2026

We collect very little, and only to run the masUNO waitlist.

What we collect

Your email address, any optional answers you give us (whether you want to find or fill games and your campus), when you signed up, how you got to the page, your public invite code and the code of the person who invited you, and your approximate country, which we estimate from your device's time zone.

How we use it

We use it to email you about masUNO launching on or near your campus, and to decide where to launch first. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in Google Sheets, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your data, or to stop getting emails, write to us at official.masuno.app@gmail.com.
