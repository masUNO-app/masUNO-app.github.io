Privacy and terms

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains what the masUNO app collects, who can see it, and the rules for using the app. For the waitlist on this website, see the waitlist privacy notice.

Who we are

masUNO helps university students find pickup basketball, tennis, soccer, volleyball and padel games that are one or two players short. You can host a game or join one on a map. We're launching at Tec de Monterrey.

We're a four-person student team, not a company. We built masUNO during the App Accelerator: Startup to 1000 (Everyone Can Code Chicago, with the City of Chicago, JPMorgan Chase and Apple). The app is published under the Boys & Girls Clubs of Chicago Apple developer team.

You can reach us at official.masuno.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in

You sign in with the account your school gave you, on your school's own sign-in page. We never see your school password. Your school's sign-in gives us:

  • Your school email address (at Tec, your @tec.mx address).
  • Your name.
  • An account ID that sign-in needs.

We get nothing else from your school account: no contacts, no files and no calendar.

Your profile

  • Your first name, last name, class year and the sports you play.
  • The dates you agreed to the community guidelines and the chat rules.
  • Your language setting.

Your games

  • Games you host: title, sport, date and time, duration, number of players, place name, the place's address and map coordinates, any meet-up spot or notes you add, and whether auto-repost is on.
  • Games you join, and when you joined or left.
  • Messages you send in a game's group chat, where messaging is available, and when you last opened each chat.

Safety

  • Reports you make: a reason, an optional note and, if you report a chat message, a copy of that message.
  • Blocks you make.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. The app asks for it to center the map and show how far games are. Your phone's location is never sent to our servers or stored by us. The only places we store are the ones hosts choose for their games. If you host a game and choose your current location as the place, that place is saved with the game like any other.
  • Saved games. Games you bookmark are saved only on your phone.
  • Your calendar. Add to calendar puts a game in your calendar only when you confirm it. We can't read your calendar.

What we don't collect

  • Ad or analytics data. There are no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking.

We don't sell your data or share it for marketing.

Crash reports reach us only through Apple, and only from people who've opted in on their iPhone.

Why we use it

  • Your school email lets you sign in and shows you're a student at your campus. We use it only for that.
  • Your name and class year show other students who's hosting or going.
  • Your game details put games on the map and let people join them.
  • Your messages let people in a game plan it together. We keep track of when you last opened a chat so we can show how many messages are new.
  • Reports and blocks help us keep masUNO safe and enforce the rules below.
  • Your language setting and the sports you play are saved with your profile. A later version will use them for Spanish and for alerts about new games.

Who sees what

  • Signed-in students at your campus can see your first name, last initial (like “Emilio G.”) and class year, the games you host with the details listed above, and the games you're going to.
  • People in the same game can also read the messages you send in its group chat and see when you joined or left.
  • No other user sees your email, your full last name or the sports you play.
  • People you block don't see your name, the games you host or your messages, and you don't see theirs.
  • Only the masUNO team sees reports.

Our team runs the database and keeps its backups, so we can see what's stored there, including group chat messages.

Where your data is stored and who helps us run masUNO

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in and group chat on servers in the United States. Access rules limit what each signed-in student can read.
  • Microsoft runs the school sign-in at Tec, through your school's own account system.
  • Apple distributes the app, provides the map and place search, and will deliver notifications in a later version.
  • Discord: when a report is made, an alert goes to our team's private channel. It has the reason, what kind of thing was reported and a report number. It doesn't include names or message text.
  • Google (Gmail) handles the email you send us.

We also keep weekly database backups in private storage.

If you choose Google Maps for directions, the game's place opens in that app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Chat messages are deleted 30 days after their game ends.

To delete your account, go to Settings › Delete account. It takes effect right away and:

  • Deletes your profile and the sign-in record that holds your name and school email.
  • Removes the games you host, along with their group chats.
  • Removes you from games you joined, and deletes the messages you sent.
  • Deletes your blocks.

Reports are kept for moderation, but they're no longer linked to your account. That covers reports you made and reports about you, including the copy of a reported message. A copy of your data may also remain in a weekly backup until that backup is replaced.

If you can't sign in, email us from your school address and we'll delete your account for you.

Community rules and safety

masUNO is for university students, not for children. To use it, you need a school account from a campus where masUNO is open.

Everyone agrees to these rules before using the app:

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate or slurs.
  • Keep it about the game: plans, timing, where to meet.
  • Don't share anyone's personal info or photos without asking.
  • Meet in public places and look out for each other.

Spam, fake games and inappropriate content aren't allowed either. Hosts can't post a game more than 7 days ahead. A text filter checks names, game titles, notes, meet-up spots and chat messages.

You can report or block anyone from the Who's going list, report a game, and report a message in a group chat. We review reports within 24 hours. We may remove content, cancel games, clear offensive names or suspend accounts.

You play at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety at games. In an emergency, call 911 (it works in Mexico and the US) or your campus security.

Coming in a later version

These features aren't in the app yet. When they arrive, if you allow notifications, we'll store a device token and your notification preferences so we can send you:

  • Game reminders.
  • Changes or cancellations to games you're in.
  • Alerts when a spot opens in a game you saved.
  • New games that match the sports you play (2 a day at most).
  • Updates on games you host.

Saved games will also be stored with your account, not only on your phone. We'll update this page when that happens.

Your choices

  • Location: say no when the app asks, or change it anytime in your iPhone's Settings. Without it, the map centers on your campus.
  • Your profile: change your name, class year and sports in the app.
  • Blocking: block anyone from the Who's going list, and undo it under Settings › Blocked people.
  • Deletion: go to Settings › Delete account.

Your rights

You can ask to access the data we have about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. These are known in Mexico as your ARCO rights. You can also withdraw your consent at any time.

To do any of this, or to ask anything about your data, email official.masuno.app@gmail.com from your school address. We'll answer within the time limits set by law.

If you think we haven't respected your rights, you can contact the Mexican authority in charge of personal data protection.
