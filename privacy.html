Privacy notice

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This notice explains what personal data masUNO collects through its waitlist, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible for your data

[Full legal name of the person or company responsible] (“masUNO”, “we”), with an address for notices at [full address in Mexico], is responsible for your personal data. You can reach us about anything in this notice at [privacy contact email].

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • Optional answers, if you choose to give them: whether you want to find games or fill them, your campus, and the sports you play.
  • Basic signup details we record automatically: the date and time you joined, and how you got to the page (for example a link from a club fair, or the website that linked you).

We don't ask for sensitive personal data, and we don't collect your name, phone number or location through this page.

Why we use it

We use your data only to:

  • add you to the masUNO waitlist;
  • email you when masUNO launches on or near your campus, and send occasional updates about that launch;
  • decide which campus and sports to launch with first, using totals across everyone who signed up.

These purposes are all needed to run the waitlist. We don't use your data for any other purpose, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Your data is stored in Google Sheets, a service provided by Google, which handles it on our behalf and only to provide that service. Google may store it on servers outside Mexico. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless a law or a competent authority requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while the waitlist is running and delete it [e.g. 12 months after we launch on your campus], or sooner if you ask us to.

Your rights

You can ask to access the data we have about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. These are known in Mexico as your ARCO rights. You can also withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop emailing you, at any time.

To do any of this, email [privacy contact email] from the address you signed up with and tell us what you'd like. We'll answer within the time limits set by law. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe.

If you think we haven't respected your rights, you can contact the Mexican authority in charge of personal data protection.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

Age

masUNO is for university students. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before joining the waitlist.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll post the new version on this page with a new date. If the change is significant, we'll also email the people on the waitlist.
