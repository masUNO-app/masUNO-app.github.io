Privacy and terms

Last updated: November 4, 2026
Effective for masUNO 1.0

This page explains what masUNO collects, who can see it, and the rules for using the app.

Who we are

masUNO helps Tec de Monterrey students find pickup basketball, tennis, soccer and volleyball games that are one or two players short. You can host a game or join one on a map.

We're a four-person student team, not a company. We built masUNO during the App Accelerator: Startup to 1000 (Everyone Can Code Chicago, with the City of Chicago, JPMorgan Chase and Apple). The app is published under the Boys & Girls Clubs of Chicago Apple developer team.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your @tec.mx email address. We email a 6-digit code to it so you can sign in, and we use it only for sign-in.
  • Your first name, last name, class year and the sports you're interested in.
  • The date you agreed to the community guidelines.
  • Your language setting.

Your games

  • Games you host: title, sport, date and time, duration, number of players, place name, the venue's address and coordinates, and any meet-up spot or notes you add.
  • Games you join.

Safety

  • Reports you make (a reason and an optional note).
  • Blocks you make.

Version 2.0 only: notifications and saved games

These features aren't in masUNO 1.0. In version 2.0, if you allow notifications, we store a device token and your notification preferences so we can send you:

  • Game reminders.
  • Changes or cancellations to games you're in.
  • Spot-opened alerts for saved games.
  • New games matching your interests (2 a day at most).
  • Updates on games you host.

Version 2.0 also stores the games you save.

What we don't collect

  • Your phone's location. The app asks for location to center the map near you, but your phone's location is never sent to our servers or stored. The only location we store is the venue a host chooses for a game.
  • Profile photos. masUNO uses initials instead.
  • Ad or analytics data. There are no ads, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

We don't sell your data or share it for marketing.

Crash reports reach us only through Apple, and only from people who've opted in on their iPhone.

Why we use it

  • Your email lets you sign in and shows you're a Tec student.
  • Your name and class year show other students who's hosting or going.
  • Your game details put games on the map and let people join them.
  • Reports and blocks help us keep masUNO safe and enforce the rules below.
  • Your language setting sets the language of the app.
  • Your interests are used in version 2.0 to tell you about new games.

Who sees what

  • Other students see only your first name, last initial (like “Emilio G.”) and class year.
  • Other signed-in Tec students can see the games you host, with all the details listed above.
  • Other students in a game you join can see that you're going.
  • No other user sees your email, your full last name or your interests.
  • Only the masUNO team sees reports.

Our team runs the database and keeps its backups, so we can see what's stored there.

Where your data is stored and who helps us run masUNO

  • Supabase runs our database and sign-in, on servers in the United States. Your data there is protected with row-level access rules.
  • Google (Gmail) sends your sign-in codes.
  • Apple distributes the app, and will deliver notifications in version 2.0.
  • Discord/Slack: when a report is made, an alert goes to our team's private channel. It contains the reason, what was reported and a link to our dashboard.

We also keep weekly database backups in private storage.

Keeping and deleting your data

Go to Settings › Delete account. This:

  • Removes your profile right away.
  • Cancels games you're hosting.
  • Removes you from games you joined.

Reports you made are kept for moderation, but they're no longer linked to you. A copy of your data may remain in the weekly backup until that backup is replaced.

Community rules and safety

To use masUNO, you must be a Tec student with a @tec.mx email.

Be respectful:

  • No harassment or hate.
  • No inappropriate content.
  • No spam or fake games.

Hosts can't post a game more than 7 days ahead. A short text filter checks names, titles, notes and meet-up spots.

You can report or block anyone from the Who's going list. We review reports within 24 hours. We may remove content, cancel games, clear offensive names or suspend accounts.

You play at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety at games. In an emergency, call 911 (it works in Mexico and the US) or Tec campus security.

Your choices

  • Location: say no when the app asks, or change it anytime in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Notifications (version 2.0 only): choose whether to allow them, and change it anytime in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Blocking: block anyone from the Who's going list. People you've blocked are listed under Blocked people.
  • Deletion: go to Settings › Delete account.

Your rights

You can ask to access the data we have about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. These are known in Mexico as your ARCO rights. You can also withdraw your consent at any time.

To do any of this, or to ask anything about your data, email official.masuno.app@gmail.com from your @tec.mx address. We'll answer within the time limits set by law.

If you think we haven't respected your rights, you can contact the Mexican authority in charge of personal data protection.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
