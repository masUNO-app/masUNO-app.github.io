Waitlist privacy notice

Last updated: November 11, 2026

We collect very little, and only to run the masUNO waitlist. For the masUNO app, see Privacy and terms.

Who we are

masUNO is made by Ricardo, Arianna, Emilio and Ethan, a four-person student team. You can reach us at official.masuno.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address, any optional answers you give us (whether you want to find or fill games and your campus), when you signed up, how you got to the page, your public invite code and the code of the person who invited you, and your approximate country, which we estimate from your device's time zone.

How we use it

We use it to email you about masUNO launching on or near your campus, and to decide where to launch first. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in Google Sheets, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. If you switch languages, your browser remembers the choice on your device.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while the waitlist is running, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access the data we have about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. These are known in Mexico as your ARCO rights. You can also withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop emailing you, at any time.

To do any of this, email official.masuno.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll answer within the time limits set by law.

If you think we haven't respected your rights, you can contact the Mexican authority in charge of personal data protection.
