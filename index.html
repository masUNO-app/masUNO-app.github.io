FAQ

Questions

Something else on your mind? Join the waitlist and reply to our first email. It goes straight to the four of us.

Why not just use GroupMe or Instagram?

Group chats only reach people you already know. That's the problem: Emilio isn't in Ricardo's group chat. masUNO is built around open spots, so a group can reach new people and you can join without having to ask.

What if I'm not good enough?

Every game says whether it's casual or competitive. And the group posted because they wanted another player, so you're arriving as someone they asked for, not someone interrupting.

Is it safe?

Everyone verifies with a university email, you can see who's hosting and who's going before you show up, and games happen in public places like campus courts and the rec center.

What about no-shows?

Everyone gets a reminder before the game. If someone drops, the host reposts the spot in one tap and everyone who bookmarked the game is notified.

I have an Android phone. Can I use it?

We're starting on iOS. Join the waitlist anyway and we'll let you know when there's a way in for you.

What's coming after launch?

We're starting with sports that need a set number of players: basketball, soccer and volleyball. Next up are club events, organizer approval, bringing a friend, and running it back with the same group every week.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to tell you when masUNO launches near you, and that's it. We don't sell it or use it for ads, and you can ask us to delete it anytime. The details are in our privacy notice.
