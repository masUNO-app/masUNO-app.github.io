FAQ

Anything else? Reply to our first email.

Why not just use a group chat?

Group chats only reach people you already know. Emilio isn't in Ricardo's.

Do I need to be good?

It's pickup, not tryouts. The game is posted because it needs one more, and that's you.

Is it safe?

Students only, public places, and you see who's going first.

What about no-shows?

When someone drops out of a full game, masUNO bumps it back to the top of the map on its own, so the open spot gets seen. Way faster than “anyone??” in the group chat.

What about Android?

We're starting on iOS. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to tell you when masUNO launches near you. We never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it anytime. Privacy notice
