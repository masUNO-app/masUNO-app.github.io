Support

Something not working, or have a question about the app? We're a small student team and we read every message.

Contact us

Email us at official.masuno.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

If you're reporting a bug, it helps to include:

  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot, if you can

Common questions

How do I join a game?

Open the map to see games near you on campus. Tap a game to see the details and who's going, then tap join and show up.

How do I post a game?

Tap Add game, fill in the sport, place, time and how many players you need, and post it. Your game shows up on the map for students nearby.

What happens if someone drops out?

When a player drops out of a full game, masUNO bumps it back to the top of the map on its own, so the open spot gets seen. This is the Auto-repost setting in the Add game form, and it's on by default.

Someone made me feel unsafe. What should I do?

If you're in danger, contact campus security or local emergency services first. You can block or report the person in the app, and you can also email us with the game and the person's name, and we'll look into it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app and delete your account there. If you can't sign in, email us from the address you use with masUNO and we'll delete it for you.

Is masUNO on Android?

Not yet. We're starting on iOS.
